---

## 1. VISION

PTNK-HUB-CLUB is the digital home of PTNK.

It is a digital community ecosystem built exclusively around the PTNK community, connecting:

* Current students
* Teachers
* Alumni / former students
* Clubs and communities

The product should become a place where PTNK members can communicate, discover information, participate in communities, build relationships, share experiences, and remain connected across generations.

HUB is not intended to be only a school website or a generic social network.

It should feel like a digital home belonging to PTNK.

---

## 2. CORE PURPOSE

PTNK-HUB-CLUB exists to strengthen the PTNK community through a shared digital environment.

The product should help the community:

* Stay connected
* Communicate naturally
* Discover relevant information
* Discover and participate in clubs
* Build friendships and relationships
* Share experiences
* Participate in activities and events
* Preserve meaningful memories
* Maintain connections between current students and alumni
* Develop a distinct PTNK digital identity

The product should prioritize genuine community value over feature quantity.

---

## 3. DIGITAL HOME OF PTNK

HUB should gradually become a common digital environment for the PTNK community.

Common communication and community information should primarily exist within HUB when appropriate.

However, external channels such as email may still be used when email is specifically required or is more appropriate for a particular purpose.

HUB should complement real-world PTNK life rather than attempt to replace it.

---

## 4. COMMUNITY ECOSYSTEM

The ecosystem should connect different parts of the PTNK community.

### 4.1 Students

Students should be able to:

* Build profiles
* Connect with other students
* Communicate
* Share content
* Discover clubs
* Participate in activities
* Build relationships
* Preserve experiences

### 4.2 Teachers

Teachers are part of the PTNK community and should be able to participate in the ecosystem.

Teacher accounts should have a recognizable identity while sharing the general basic platform permission baseline unless specific rules define otherwise.

Teacher content does not follow the student-to-alumni Memories lifecycle.

### 4.3 Alumni / OSTUDENT

Graduated students should remain connected to the PTNK community through the OSTUDENT identity.

Their previous student experiences should remain part of PTNK's long-term history.

They may continue participating in the community while maintaining their alumni identity.

### 4.4 Clubs

Clubs should exist as active community spaces rather than static information pages.

A club should be able to:

* Present its identity
* Share information
* Publish content
* Organize activities
* Communicate with members
* Build a community around its interests

Club leaders and authorized club roles should have meaningful control over their club's presence within the boundaries defined by product rules.

---

## 5. SOCIAL ECOSYSTEM

HUB should support meaningful social interaction within the PTNK community.

The long-term ecosystem may include:

* Profiles
* Follow relationships
* Friend relationships
* Direct communication
* Club/community communication
* Posts
* Notes
* Stories
* Reactions and interactions
* Club activities
* Events
* Notifications
* Other community-oriented experiences

These capabilities should serve the community rather than exist only for feature completeness.

---

## 6. NOTES

Notes are a lightweight form of personal expression.

A Note may contain:

* Text
* Optional music

Notes do not require image attachments.

Notes are associated with the user's profile/avatar and are intended primarily for Friends at the vision level.

Detailed visibility and operational rules are defined elsewhere.

---

## 7. PTNK MEMORIES

PTNK Memories are a long-term preservation system for meaningful experiences within the PTNK community.

The goal is to prevent valuable student experiences from disappearing when students graduate.

### Student lifecycle

During student life:

```text
STUDENT
   ↓
Active HUB participation
   ↓
Graduation
   ↓
OSTUDENT
```

Existing student content should remain preserved as part of PTNK Memories after the user becomes an alumni / OSTUDENT.

### Alumni content

New content created by alumni remains active for a defined period before entering the Memories system.

The exact period is defined by Product Rules.

### Teacher content

Teacher content does not enter the student-to-alumni Memories lifecycle.

### Memory principle

Memories should preserve the historical continuity of PTNK while respecting the original privacy and visibility of the content.

---

## 8. CROSS-GENERATION CONTINUITY

HUB should connect different generations of the PTNK community.

The system should make it possible for:

```text
Current Students
      ↓
Shared experiences
      ↓
Graduation
      ↓
OSTUDENT
      ↓
Preserved memories
      ↓
Continued connection
```

The goal is not simply to retain old data.

The goal is to preserve a sense of community continuity.

A student should be able to remain part of PTNK even after leaving school.

---

## 9. COMMUNITY IDENTITY

PTNK-HUB-CLUB should have a recognizable identity that belongs to PTNK.

The product should avoid becoming:

* A generic social network
* A generic school portal
* A collection of disconnected features
* A visually generic AI-generated website

The design, interactions, language, content structure, and community experiences should contribute to a distinct PTNK identity.

The product should feel intentional rather than templated.

---

## 10. USER CONTROL AND PRIVACY

Users should have meaningful control over:

* Their profile
* Their content
* Their relationships
* Their visibility
* Their participation

The system should respect user privacy and avoid exposing personal information beyond what is necessary for the product and what the user has permitted.

Privacy should be treated as part of the product experience, not only as a technical requirement.

Detailed privacy and visibility rules are defined in Product Rules.

---

## 11. SAFETY AND COMMUNITY HEALTH

HUB should provide an environment where members can participate safely and responsibly.

The product should support appropriate mechanisms for:

* Reporting
* Moderation
* Blocking
* Handling violating content
* Protecting reporter privacy
* Administrative intervention when necessary

Safety mechanisms should protect the community without unnecessarily damaging normal community interaction.

Detailed moderation rules and operational procedures are defined elsewhere.

---

## 12. ADMINISTRATION

Administration exists to maintain the health and operation of the platform.

Administrators may manage:

* Users
* Permissions
* Reports
* Violations
* Platform content actions
* Authorized administrative data

However, administration should not automatically mean ownership of the product or unilateral control over product direction.

Ownership, administration, development, and data management may be separate responsibilities.

---

## 13. DEVELOPMENT AND CONTINUITY

PTNK-HUB-CLUB should be designed as a long-term product rather than a temporary school assignment.

The project should be maintainable, understandable, and transferable when necessary.

The product should be able to continue evolving even as the original student developers graduate.

Development decisions should therefore consider:

* Maintainability
* Documentation
* Knowledge transfer
* Developer succession
* Data continuity
* Product continuity

The project should preserve its identity while allowing future generations to continue building it.

---

## 14. OWNERSHIP AND RESPONSIBILITY

Ownership, administration, development, and data management should be treated as distinct concepts.

The Founder currently owns the product.

Future ownership may be transferred under appropriate conditions, including possible future transfer to the school.

Such transfer is not automatic.

Detailed ownership and succession rules are defined in Product Rules.

---

## 15. LONG-TERM EVOLUTION

HUB should evolve based on:

* Real community needs
* User feedback
* Observed problems
* Practical opportunities
* The long-term identity of PTNK

New technologies or trends should not be adopted simply because they are popular.

The product should adapt when useful while preserving its core identity.

---

## 16. ADAPT, NOT COPY

PTNK-HUB-CLUB may learn from existing digital products and communities.

However, it should not simply copy another platform.

Existing patterns may be studied for:

* Usability
* Interaction ideas
* Information architecture
* Community mechanics
* Technical approaches

But the final experience should be adapted to PTNK's own community, culture, needs, and identity.

---

## 17. VISION BOUNDARY

This document defines the long-term vision and identity of PTNK-HUB-CLUB.

It does **not** define:

* Programming languages
* Frameworks
* Databases
* APIs
* Infrastructure
* Deployment architecture
* Folder structures
* Detailed UI specifications
* Exact moderation workflows
* Detailed permission matrices
* Detailed data retention periods
* Implementation-specific technical decisions

Those decisions belong to lower-level documents where appropriate.
